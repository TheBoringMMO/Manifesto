# Manifesto

This is the initial manifesto for the boring mmo.
Why is it the boring mmo? that's because this mmo
is going to try to do the right thing for the players
by trying to be fair as fuck. The expectation is that 
if the mmo is balanced in as many ways as possible 
it will be dull as fuck. Secondly it's also boring because
I have not theme or story in mind. just systems and
rules to keep it all together.


# goals

1. make every thing you do in the mmo progress at the same pace
ie: xp per hour or gold per hour consistent as possible
2. do not limit socialization ie:, markets, servers, sharding
all these technical requirements for a positive experience
should not be a restriction
3. respect a players time. the time spent playing should
be positive and engaging

# Game Mechanics

The core mechanics of the game will focus on providing a balanced and fair experience for all players. This includes consistent progression rates, equal opportunities for all players, and a focus on skill-based gameplay rather than pay-to-win mechanics.

# Economy

The in-game economy will be designed to be stable and fair. Only 1 currency for will be earned through gameplay activities. The economy will be player-driven, with a focus on trade and resource management.
Currency will be exchangeble for game time. The grey market will always exist so offer a low friction alternative.
Bartering will exist to support currency free trades.

# Combat System

The combat system will be skill-based, with an emphasis on strategy and tactics. Players will have access to a variety of abilities and weapons, and combat will be continually balanced to ensure that no single strategy or playstyle is dominant.

# Account Progression

Progression with be account based.

# Social Features

The game will include a variety of social features to encourage player interaction and community building. This includes in-game chat, guilds, and player-driven events. The goal is to create a positive and engaging social experience for all players.
